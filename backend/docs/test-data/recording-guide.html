FARQ: recording guide for the enrolled team member

Team Off-Grid · Amazon AI Cyber Security Hackathon · about 30–40 minutes of your time

FARQ checks a forwarded video against genuine recordings of the person in it. For our demo, you are that person. We need genuine videos of you speaking, so FARQ can learn your normal face, voice and movement, and so we can make test fakes from them on-site.

1. Consent (record this first)

Record a short video named consent-video-<your initials>.mp4 saying:

“My name is ____. I agree that Team Off-Grid may record videos of my face and voice, use them to build and test FARQ during this hackathon, and make test fake videos from them on-site, which will only be shown at this event. All of it will be deleted afterwards.”

2. Set-up (same for every clip)

  • Phone camera in landscape (sideways), 1080p, at eye level, about 1 metre away.
  • Face and shoulders in frame, looking at the camera like a news statement.
  • Even light on your face: face a window or lamp, no bright light behind you.
  • Quiet room, no fan or music. Lean the phone on something so it stays still.
  • Plain background is best. Keep the same place for all clips.

3. How to speak

  • Speak naturally, in Hindi, English or both, the way you normally talk.
  • Move your head and eyebrows as you normally do. Don't hold still. FARQ checks that head and eyebrow movement fits the voice, so natural movement matters.
  • No script needed. Small slips are fine: keep going, don't restart.
  • Each clip 30 to 90 seconds.
  • Don't mention real investment schemes, real phone numbers or real UPI IDs.

5. Optional, but it makes the demo much stronger: a mock scam pitch

Record pitch-01-scam.mp4 in the same set-up, reading the script below like a confident advertisement. Everything in it is made up: the person, the organisation, the number, the UPI ID and the website (example.com is reserved for examples). Never dial the number. Change the name and the verb forms (e.g. “raha hoon / rahi hoon”) to however you'd naturally say it.

Namaste doston. Main Rakesh Mehra, National Wealth Board ki taraf se baat kar raha hoon. Aaj main aapko ek special scheme ke baare mein bataunga.
Is scheme mein aapko guaranteed forty percent return milega, sirf teen mahine mein. Yeh offer sirf is hafte tak hai, toh jaldi kijiye.
Register karne ke liye hamare WhatsApp group mein join kijiye. Ya seedha call kijiye: nine eight seven six five, four three two one zero.
Payment ke liye UPI ID hai: farq demo at ok fake bank. Ek baar phir: farq demo at ok fake bank.
Zyada jaankari ke liye website dekhiye: bonus dot example dot com.
Yaad rakhiye, aisa mauka baar baar nahi aata. Dhanyavaad.

If you can, record it twice: pitch-01-scam.mp4 as above, and pitch-02-scam-english.mp4 reading the same pitch in English. Each takes about 45 seconds.

6. Sending the files

  • Do not send them over WhatsApp. WhatsApp shrinks videos, and we need the originals.
  • Copy them straight off the phone (USB cable) or upload the original files to Google Drive and share the folder with the team lead.
  • Keep the file names as listed above.

7. What happens to your videos

  • Most of the clips go into FARQ's library as your “genuine recordings”. 2 to 4 are kept aside to test FARQ on videos it hasn't seen.
  • Test fakes are made on-site with video-editing tools: for example, the picture from one of your clips with the voice from the scam pitch. They are shown only at this event.
  • The files stay on the team laptop, are never uploaded to GitHub, and are deleted after the event.
